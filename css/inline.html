About Cities

Manali | Kolkata | Banglore

Manali

Manali is a popular high-altitude resort town in Himachal Pradesh, India, set along the Beas River. Known for snow-capped peaks, pine forests, and adventure sports, it sits in the Kullu Valley about 544 km northeast of New Delhi.

Kolkata

Kolkata is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of West Bengal. It is located on the east bank of the Hooghly River, a distributary of the Ganges, and is known for its rich cultural heritage, colonial architecture, and vibrant street life.

Banglore

Bangalore, also known as Bengaluru, is the capital and largest city of the Indian state of Karnataka. It is located in the southern part of the state and is known for its technology industry, beautiful gardens, and pleasant climate.
